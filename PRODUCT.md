# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and everyday users scanning printed documents or handwritten notes from an Android phone. They need a fast, private, offline workflow while holding the phone over a page or importing a screenshot.

## Product Purpose

N152 captures an image, detects and corrects document perspective when appropriate, and extracts editable text on-device. The primary task is camera-first scanning. Results should remain understandable as a document, preserving line breaks and the visual rhythm of the source as much as the OCR engine allows.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, professional, dependable. The interface should feel focused and calm during scanning, with clear feedback and no decorative distractions.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI dashboards, crowded control panels, excessive rounded cards, decorative gradients, novelty illustrations, and all-caps instructional copy. Avoid making gallery import feel equal to the primary camera workflow.

## Design Principles

- Camera first: the scan action is the clearest and most prominent next step.
- Quiet confidence: use restrained color, strong contrast, and precise feedback.
- Document fidelity: present extracted text as a readable document, not only as a plain textarea.
- Offline clarity: make processing state and on-device behavior easy to understand.
- Progressive disclosure: keep advanced modes such as handwriting available without competing with the primary scan path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, touch targets of at least 48dp, descriptive labels, visible processing and error states, and layouts that remain usable with large font settings. Avoid relying on color alone to communicate scan status.
