[40 s] Before any reasoning, the agent has to perceive. OpenCV finds the page with Canny edges and the largest four-sided contour covering at least 15% of the frame, then warps it flat. A reflex rule checks brightness and blur and tells the user to retake. CLAHE boosts contrast. Then two recognisers: ML Kit for print, and a PP-OCRv5 model in ONNX Runtime for handwriting, which falls back to ML Kit when its confidence is below 0.20. Finally, layout is rebuilt from box geometry. The output of all this is the agent's percept: ordered lines of noisy text.
