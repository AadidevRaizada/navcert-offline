TIME: 45 seconds. SAY: Ships go into dry dock to be straightened and cleaned before they sail again. Documents get the same treatment here. SAY: Row one finds the page on a sixteen-hundred-pixel copy: grayscale, Gaussian blur, Canny, a morphological close, then the biggest convex quadrilateral. In parallel, brightness and Laplacian variance decide whether to warn the user. SAY: Row two cleans the page at up to thirty-two hundred pixels: perspective warp, CLAHE, a median filter, then sharpening, or adaptive thresholding in black-and-white mode, and a white border. SAY: Only then does OCR run, all on the phone. Every number on this slide is a constant in DocumentScanner.kt. CUE: row one, pause, row two, pause, the blue OCR tail. BACK-POCKET: Why two resolutions? Border detection needs structure, OCR needs stroke detail. The app only downsamples; it never upsamples a small photo.
