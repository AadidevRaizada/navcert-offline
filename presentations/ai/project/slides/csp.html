[50 s] Today the app checks each field alone. Nothing stops it reporting an expiry date before the issue date, for example when the two labels are read in the wrong order. That's a constraint satisfaction problem. Variables: the five fields. Domains: the candidates the rules found, plus abstain. Constraints: expiry after issue, port in a gazetteer, authority that looks like an organisation. I wrote a backtracking solver with MRV ordering and forward checking, with nine unit tests. On 2,000 synthetic cases, whole-record accuracy rose from 16 to 86 percent. But look at the last number: when a wrong date still satisfies every constraint, the CSP fixes none of them. Constraints catch contradictions, not plausible mistakes. This is an experiment; adding it to the app is the next step.
