04

[50 s] In course terms, NavCert is a rational agent. Performance: copy fields exactly, and abstain rather than invent. A blank is better than a wrong expiry date. Environment: a real paper certificate under real light. Actuators: guidance, a flattened page, text, fields, answers. Sensors: the camera and my touches. The environment is partially observable, because glare and stamps hide text. It's stochastic, because two photos of the same page give different OCR. It's episodic: each certificate stands alone. It's static while processing, continuous in pixels but discrete in its output, and single-agent. The agent itself is layered: a simple reflex rule for capture quality, a model-based rule system for fields, and a utility-maximiser that picks the highest-scoring answer span.
