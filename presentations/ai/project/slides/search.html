[50 s] OCR post-correction looks like spell-check, but in AI terms it's a search problem. The app today does generate-and-test: it compares the word with all 206 dictionary words and accepts the best only if it clearly wins. I reformulated it properly. The state is how much of the noisy word I've read and where I am in a dictionary trie. A known OCR confusion like rn read for m costs 0.4; any other edit costs 1. Uniform-cost search finds the cheapest word. A* adds an admissible heuristic from the length mismatch, and on my synthetic test it expands about three times fewer nodes for the same answers. The experiment also caught a real bug: the app skips any word with a digit, so its own zero-to-o confusion map never runs. That finding is documented, and the fix is planned.
