TIME: 35 seconds. SAY: This is a synthetic certificate, every name on it is invented, photographed the way a seafarer actually does it. CLICK through the four circles: One, the page is a trapezium, not a rectangle. Two, the light falls off across the page and there is a shadow. Three, a little hand-shake blur and sensor noise. Four, a red stamp sitting on top of the text. SAY: And on a ship, or in a port office, there is often no signal to send this to a cloud OCR service. These are private documents anyway. Even the IMO guidance for electronic certificates assumes the verifier has internet. BACK-POCKET: Why synthetic? The real corpus is a client's private certificates; I never put them in slides or the paper. BACK-POCKET: What is the source on IMO? MSC.1/Circ.1665, 28 June 2023, guidelines on the use of electronic certificates of seafarers.
