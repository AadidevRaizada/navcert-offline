AADIDEV RAIZADA · N152 · MOBILE APPLICATION DEVELOPMENT · NMIMS MPSTME

02

[25 s] The project is marked out of 40 on five criteria. Originality of the problem, user needs and the technical aspects of the tools carry 5 marks and are covered on slides 3 to 7. Self-learning, meaning new components beyond the syllabus, carries 5 and is on slide 11, with the syllabus mapping on slide 10. A standardized working application, Material Design and Play Store requirements, carries 10 and is on slides 8, 9, 12 and 13. Individual contribution with evidence carries 10, slide 14. The viva, presentation and demo carry 10, slides 15 and 16. Each slide carries a small label naming the criterion it serves.
