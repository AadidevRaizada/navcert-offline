[50 s] The field extractor is a classic rule-based expert system. Knowledge base: patterns I acquired by reading the corpus, label phrases, a date grammar, organisation words, and one important negative rule. A battery or equipment date is never the certificate's expiry. Inference engine: a gate first. If the page has no certificate cue, nothing fires, so meeting notes never get fake fields. Then each field has an ordered rule chain; the first rule that proves it wins. That's conflict resolution by priority, plus recency: the last Issued at line beats an attachment's. Chaining: the name rule asserts a title, and the authority rule then fires on the header above that title. That's forward chaining. Fallback: only when rules fail and there's evidence, it asks a learned model, and validates the answer. The port field never asks the model at all.
