03

[45 s] Five groups touch these documents. The seafarer needs them valid to sign on; the BIMCO and ICS 2026 workforce report counts about 2.57 million STCW-certified seafarers. The manning agent checks a whole crew's certificates before deployment. The ship manager tracks vessel certificates and their expiry across a fleet. Inspectors read them on board. Training institutes issue them and answer verification calls. Two constraints cut across all of them: connectivity at sea and in many ports is limited, and IMO's own guidance on electronic certificates assumes the verifier has an Internet connection. These documents also carry identity data, so uploading them to a cloud OCR service is a privacy risk. So the requirement became: everything on the device, nothing over the network.
