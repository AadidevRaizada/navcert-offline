TIME: 50 seconds. Units 1, 4, 5, 6. SAY: Step one, find the page. A 5 by 5 Gaussian removes paper grain, then Canny. The high threshold, 170, only starts edges on strong contrast like the page border; hysteresis lets them continue down to 55 through the shadow. SAY: Canny edges still break. A 5 by 5 closing, dilation then erosion, bridges those breaks, so the contour is closed. SAY: Contours are simplified to polygons; I keep the largest convex four-sided one covering at least 15 percent of the frame. SAY: Four corners give eight equations, exactly the eight unknowns of a homography. warpPerspective does the rest. CUE: the animation walks through the numbered lines: edges, gaps closing, four corners, warp. It ends on the app's status text, 'Page borders found and aligned'. BACK-POCKET: If no quad is found, the app keeps the whole image and the user can crop manually in the crop editor. BACK-POCKET: Corner ordering uses x+y and y-x; it can confuse corners on a page rotated near 45 degrees, which is in my limitations.
