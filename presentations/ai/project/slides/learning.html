[50 s] Learning. I had no hand labels, so I used weak supervision: my own rules and OCR pseudo-labels became the teacher. To avoid leakage I split by document, never by page, because pages of one certificate share names and dates. The first model, BERT-tiny, reached 23.5 percent test exact match. Too weak. A fine-tuned MiniLM reached 71 percent on weak validation but 15 of 26, 57.7 percent, on six human-checked gold documents. That gap is the lesson: on weak labels the model also learns the teacher's mistakes, so the human-checked number is the one to trust. Quantised to int8 it shrinks to 32 MiB and keeps 15 of 26. I use exact match because a date with one wrong digit is a wrong date.
