03

[45 s] Every seafarer and every ship runs on certificates: competency, medical fitness, safety, insurance. The BIMCO and ICS 2026 report counts about 2.57 million STCW-certified seafarers. Port state control checks these papers: the Tokyo MOU recorded crew-certificate deficiencies rising from 877 in 2022 to 1,304 in 2024. In India, about 80 percent of seafarers work on foreign-flagged ships, and the DG Shipping circular of July 2025 puts the verification duty on recruitment agencies. Yet the IMO guidance on electronic certificates assumes the verifying party has an Internet connection. In practice the documents travel as phone photos, taken at an angle, in poor light. A generic scanner fixes the picture but not the meaning. My problem statement: read the certificate on the phone, extract its key fields, and answer questions, offline and privately.
