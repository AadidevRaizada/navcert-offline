[40 s] Here's the paradox. The documents we most want to protect, identity and competency certificates, are exactly the ones we usually push to a cloud OCR service. That creates two problems: it fails when there is no signal, and it moves personal data off the device. So the design constraint was strict: the manifest removes the internet permission entirely. Every AI component you'll see today, OCR, rules, transformers, runs on the phone. That constraint shapes every algorithm choice that follows.
