[45 s] The capture screen makes the camera the main action and keeps gallery import secondary. You choose Auto, Printed or Handwriting. If the frame is messy, the crop editor lets you crop and rotate; it is a third-party CropImageView hosted inside Compose with AndroidView. Then OpenCV takes over: edge detection, closing gaps, finding the largest four-cornered shape that covers at least fifteen percent of the frame, and warping it flat. The preview also checks if the photo is too dark, too bright or blurry, and offers three enhancement modes. The page in these screenshots is a synthetic certificate generated for testing, never a real one.
