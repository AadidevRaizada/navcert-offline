[45 s] The learned part is a transformer doing extractive question answering. It reads the question and the certificate text together, and for every token predicts two probabilities: is this where the answer starts, and is this where it ends. The best span wins. That's a search too: the top 12 starts times the top 12 ends, capped at 32 tokens. The key property is that it can only highlight text that exists. It cannot invent a date. For asking questions across saved documents, text is cut into 1,200-character chunks with 180 characters of overlap, so an answer on a boundary is never cut in half. The best four chunks go to MobileBERT.
