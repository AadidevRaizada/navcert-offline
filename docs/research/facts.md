Notes for use on slides:

- Rows 10 to 13 come from the IMO Secretariat paper STW 32/6 for the 32nd session of the STW Sub-Committee. The PDF metadata dates the file 22 November 2000; the figures describe the five years before the study, and the paper itself says the low detection rate points to weak control mechanisms worldwide. Describe them as "an IMO research study around 2000", not as current statistics.
- Row 6 is reported by Marine Insight from the BIMCO-ICS 2026 report. The BIMCO executive summary (row 1) confirms India ranks second among supplier nations but does not print the Indian headcount in the text extracted for this research.
- Row 16 is a secondary summary; the Paris MoU report page itself could not be opened during research.
- The project's own measured results (corpus size, model accuracy) are in `ml/README.md` and are not repeated here.
