[40 s] NavCert Offline does four things. Scan: it finds the page in the photo, flattens it and cleans it. Read: it recognises printed text, and handwriting through a second model. Extract: it pulls out the five fields people actually retype: certificate name, issue date, port of issue, issuing authority and expiry date. Ask: you can ask a question in plain English across your saved documents. No account, no server, and the network permission is removed from the manifest.
