[35 s] Ships and seafarers carry many certificates, and the first things anyone checks are five facts: what is it, when was it issued, where, by whom, and when does it expire. I was given a real corpus: 108 PDFs, 351 pages, and 221 of those pages are images with no text layer at all. And ships are often out of signal range. So the question for this project was: can a phone do this job alone, offline, and be honest about what it does not know?
